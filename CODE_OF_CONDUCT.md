# Code of Conduct

HYSYS Companion is a tool for process engineers. We want every issue, review and
discussion around it to stay useful and respectful, whatever your experience,
language (Korean or English), employer or background.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are engineers first and software users
  second.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with a change, a value or a
  design, and explain why.
- Assume good faith. A short or translated message is usually not rude on purpose.
- Keep company secrets out of public places. Do not post confidential case files,
  plant data, customer names or internal paths in issues. Remove them from logs
  and screenshots first.
- Respect a "no". If someone asks you to stop, stop.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discrimination based on any personal characteristic.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or reviews after being asked to stop.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when
you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile
[github.com/wjgoarxiv](https://github.com/wjgoarxiv). Please do not use a public
issue to report a conduct problem. Reports are kept private, and the reporter's
identity is not shared without consent.

## What happens next

The maintainer reviews every report and may respond with a private note, a
public warning, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the
project's spaces, depending on what happened. Decisions are explained to the
people involved.
